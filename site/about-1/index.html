Night Vision AMAZON BEST SELLER

How to See Your Way Through Any Situation

I'm Dr. Kem, and I am excited about my book written especially for you, for this specific moment. You would be surprised to know it had been written for a while — but God burdened me to release it NOW.

Though current circumstances may seem dark, chaotic or blurred, you WILL see your way through. We must be willing to change our perspective on the way we view our “unpleasant” situations — or current darkness, if you will.

Through an unexpected and traumatic night of being lost in the woods, Dr. McKenzie emerged with twenty-one life-altering revelations that will change anyone's perspective about seemingly destitute situations. Let NIGHT VISION serve as your compass through the dark moments life may set before you.

K.E.M. — Keep Everything Moving

Letting Adversity Guide You To Destiny

In K.E.M., Dr. McKenzie shares her story in the belief that others will be encouraged to celebrate adversity on their way to destiny. Coupled with the Study Guide Journal — designed to let you delve into each chapter a little further — you can stop, look at your life, and derive a plan to face adverse situations.
