MEET YOUR MIND DOCTOR

She is the answer
you've been looking for.

Dr. Kemberly E. McKenzie is a certified life coach with extensive experience helping clients achieve their personal and professional goals — skilled in active listening, empathy, and collaborative problem-solving, and committed to empowering individuals to reach their full potential and live fulfilling lives.

With a doctorate in Educational Leadership, four master's degrees—including a Master of Arts in Clinical Mental Health Counseling—and over 30 years of experience in education spanning Philadelphia, Chicago, and Metro Atlanta, Dr. Kemberly E. McKenzie brings a unique blend of expertise in education, leadership, and mental health. As an Associate Professional Counselor (APC) and National Certified Counselor (NCC), she integrates her extensive educational background with her clinical training to support personal growth, emotional well-being, and professional development. Her additional credentials include certifications as a Behavior Specialist, Anger Management Specialist, True Colors Facilitator, and Professional Development Life Coach. Her academic journey also includes international study experiences at the prestigious universities of Cambridge and Oxford.
