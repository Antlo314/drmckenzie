Credentials

Dr. McKenzie holds a Bachelor's degree in Speech Communication, four Master's degrees in Elementary Education, Special Education, Educational Leadership, and Clinical Mental Health Counseling, and a Doctorate in Educational Leadership. She is also an Associate Professional Counselor (APC) and National Certified Counselor (NCC). Her academic journey includes international study experiences at the prestigious universities of Cambridge and Oxford.

Experience

Extensive experience in Education, Leadership Development, Team Building, and Staff and Professional Development, with over 30 years of service as an educator across several states, from Philadelphia and Chicago to the Atlanta Metro area.

Ministry & travel

Dr. McKenzie has traveled the world spreading the Gospel and providing clothing and food to children and families in need. This work has taken her to the following regions: Barcelona, Paris, Rome, London, the Dominican Republic, and Cape Town, South Africa.

She is the author of K.E.M. (Keep Everything Moving): Letting Adversity Guide You to Destiny, Amazon's best seller, Night Vision: How to See Your Way Through Any Situation, and Mind Your Manuscript: A Writing Guide for Emerging Authors, released in April 2026.
