Meet your counselor.

Dr. McKenzie holds a doctorate in Educational Leadership and four master's degrees, including a Master of Arts in Clinical Mental Health Counseling. Her background in education and leadership informs her compassionate approach to personal growth and emotional well-being.

Her credentials include Associate Professional Counselor (APC), National Certified Counselor (NCC), Behavior Specialist, Anger Management Specialist, True Colors Facilitator, and Professional Development Life Coach, with additional training in trauma and Christian sex therapy.
