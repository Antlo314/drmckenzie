Life happens. Let’s talk about it.

Kemberly E. McKenzie, PhD, APC, NCC, is a trauma-informed therapist and National Certified Counselor who helps individuals navigate anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, life transitions, relationship concerns, and stress-related challenges. Drawing on 30 years of experience in education, leadership, and advocacy, she combines evidence-based practices with a compassionate, client-centered approach.

Dr. Kem uses Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Person-Centered Therapy, trauma-informed care, and solution-focused techniques to help clients develop insight, build resilience, and create meaningful change.

She also has specialized training in sex therapy from a Christian perspective and helps clients explore concerns related to intimacy, sexuality, relationships, faith, and emotional wellness. Dr. Kem welcomes clients of all backgrounds and beliefs and can integrate faith and spirituality into counseling when the client wishes.

Therapy can be a place to pause, be heard, gain clarity, and develop practical tools to move forward with greater confidence and emotional wellness.

Services and payment
